  • July 9, 2026: Travel Support Applications Now Open

    PACT 2026 is pleased to offer travel support to students and early-career researchers who wish to attend the conference and its affiliated workshops. Travel awards are intended to help broaden participation by supporting attendees who will benefit from engaging with the PACT community, presenting their work, and participating in the conference program.

    Maximum reimbursement amounts:

    • Up to USD $800 for domestic participants studying or working in the United States.
    • Up to USD $1,400 for international participants.
    Travel support is competitive and subject to available funding. We encourage undergraduate students, graduate students, and early-career researchers (within three years of their Ph.D.) to apply.
    Application deadline: 9/30/2026
    Apply here: Travel Support Application Form

  • May 6, 2026: PACT 2026 Tutorials Are Now Available Online

    PACT 2026 is pleased to announce that the tutorial program is now available online. The tutorials will take place on Monday, October 19, 2026, and will offer attendees the opportunity to engage with timely topics, tools, and methods in parallel architectures, compilation techniques, and related areas. We encourage participants to review the tutorial offerings and plan their PACT 2026 attendance accordingly. Tutorials are included at no additional fee with either full conference registration or one-day registration for Monday, October 19. The full tutorial program is available here: https://pact2026.github.io/workshops
